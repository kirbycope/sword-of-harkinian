Sword Kirby for Ship of Harkinian 1.1.1

Sword Kirby replaces Link, child and adult, in Ship of Harkinian 9.3.0.

Install
1. Copy kirby_child.o2r and kirby_adult.o2r into Ship of Harkinian's "mods" folder,
   the one beside oot.o2r that holds custom_mod_files_go_here.txt.
2. Start the game. There is nothing to enable.
3. Delete the two files to get Link back.

Optional
kirby_voice.o2r gives him Kirby's voice (from Super Smash Bros.) in place of Link's.
Copy it into the same folder, or leave it out.

What it does
- Kirby's body, cap and shoes on Link's skeleton, with his face and blinking eyes.
- The sheathed sword and shield ride on the back of the ball; the drawn sword is held
  blade up beside him and the shield comes up at his side.
- All 573 player animations are re-posed so his mittens stay on his body, and the pause
  screen's Equipment page is posed to match.
- He sits in the saddle, standing and at a gallop.

New in 1.1.1
- His sword hand no longer draws a second mitten over the game's own closed hand, which
  showed as a lump hanging off his side with the sword put away.

New in 1.1.0
- The model is rebuilt with Harbour Masters' own Fast64 fork, the port's supported
  tooling, instead of resources written by hand. The archive now ships a real skeleton.
- Fixes a crash. Every paletted texture was asking the port to load 256 palette entries
  out of palettes holding four to eight, which killed the game on some scenes, Link's
  House among them.
- His hands are his own again in every pose, rather than Link's hands in Kirby's colour.
- He no longer rides on the horse's neck or its rump.

Known limits
- Held items other than the sword and shield (bottles, ocarina, slingshot, bow) sit
  where Link's hand is, which on a ball this size is beside the cap.
- The cap's tail pokes through the sheathed shield from some angles.
- Built and played on Windows (DirectX 11). 1.0.0 also ran on macOS (Metal); this build
  has not been checked there yet.
