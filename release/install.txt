Sword Kirby for Ship of Harkinian 1.0.0

Sword Kirby replaces Link, child and adult, in Ship of Harkinian 9.3.0.

Install
1. Copy kirby_child.o2r and kirby_adult.o2r into Ship of Harkinian's "mods" folder,
   the one beside oot.o2r that holds custom_mod_files_go_here.txt.
2. Start the game. There is nothing to enable.
3. Delete the two files to get Link back.

Optional, built by you
A voice mod gives him Kirby's voice in place of Link's. It is not in this zip because it
is made from your own Super Smash Bros. ROM: see tools/kirby_voice_map.py in the source
repository below.

What it does
- Kirby's body, cap and shoes on Link's skeleton, with his face and blinking eyes.
- The sheathed sword and shield ride on the back of the ball; the drawn sword is held
  blade up beside him and the shield comes up at his side.
- All 573 player animations are re-posed so his mittens stay on his body, and the pause
  screen's Equipment page is posed to match.
- Works on Windows (DirectX 11) and macOS (Metal).

Known limits
- Held items other than the sword and shield (bottles, ocarina, slingshot, bow) sit
  where Link's hand is, which on a ball this size is beside the cap.
- The cap's tail pokes through the sheathed shield from some angles.
