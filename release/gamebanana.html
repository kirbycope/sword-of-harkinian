Sword Kirby replaces Link, child and adult, in Ship of Harkinian 9.3.0.

Kirby's sword ability is already Link in everything but shape: he wears the green cap and swings a blade. This mod puts the ball underneath. His body, cap and shoes ride Link's skeleton with his face and blinking eyes. The sheathed sword and shield sit on the back of the ball, the drawn sword is held blade up beside him, the shield comes up at his side, and all 573 of Link's animations are re-posed so his mittens stay on his body instead of swinging around on Link's arms. The pause screen's Equipment page is posed to match. Both ages, every sword and shield.

Works on Windows (DirectX 11) and macOS (Metal).

Install

  1. Copy kirby_child.o2r and kirby_adult.o2r into Ship of Harkinian's mods folder (the one beside oot.o2r that holds custom_mod_files_go_here.txt).
  2. Start the game. Nothing to enable.
  3. Delete the two files to get Link back.

kirby_voice.o2r is optional and gives him Kirby's voice from Super Smash Bros. in place of Link's.

Known limits

  • Held items other than the sword and shield (bottles, ocarina, slingshot, bow) sit where Link's hand is, which on a ball this size is beside the cap.
  • The cap's tail pokes through the sheathed shield from some angles.

Source

The build scripts, and how a custom player model gets into this port at all, are at github.com/kirbycope/sword-of-harkinian. Two port bugs found on the way are filed upstream and worked around here: Metal crashes on any skeleton limb served from a mod, and animation data served in place of the game's own mis-draws the player.
